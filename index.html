About MyMovie

MyMovie is a movie planning application that helps users discover movies, explore different genres and organise their favourite movies in a personal watchlist.

Application Features

  • Search movies by title.
  • Discover movies based on genres.
  • Add movies to My Watchlist.
  • Delete movies from My Watchlist.
  • Calculate total movie watching duration.
